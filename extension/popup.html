What this does (plain words)

When you click Send, this extension reads two cookies that ESPN already put in your browser when you signed in (espn_s2 and SWID) and opens your scoreboard's setup page with them, over your home Wi-Fi. The scoreboard uses them to read your fantasy leagues, the same way the ESPN website does.

It never sees or asks for your ESPN password. It sends nothing to anyone but the scoreboard address you typed. It does nothing in the background and has no access to other websites. The source code is public and short enough to read in a few minutes.

ESPN's sign-in cookie lasts about a year. When the board's setup page says the ESPN sign-in has expired, sign in to espn.com again and click Send again.
